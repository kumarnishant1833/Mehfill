Our Story

A Taste of Elegance in Basti

Nestled in the heart of Basti, Mehfill Fine Dining was born from a passion for exceptional food and unforgettable experiences. We believe dining is more than a meal — it is a celebration of flavours, warmth, and togetherness.

From our stunning rooftop to our thoughtfully crafted interiors, every corner of Mehfill is designed to create moments worth remembering. Whether you're celebrating a birthday, a family gathering, or simply craving restaurant-quality flavours — Mehfill welcomes you.

Our chefs bring the finest North Indian spices, Chinese authenticity, Italian-style pizzas, and crafted mocktails to your table — served with the warmth of Uttar Pradesh hospitality.
